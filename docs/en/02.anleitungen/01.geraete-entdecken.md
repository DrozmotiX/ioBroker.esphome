## Why does auto-discovery report routers or foreign devices and how do I exclude an IP?

<!-- abschnitt: prio=B; gewicht=7; readme=missing; cluster=connection-setup-13; features=discovery-mdns,ignore-list,autodiscovery-setting,discovery-new-device-list -->
<!-- muss beantworten: Ignorierliste im Bereich Erkennung der Haupteinstellungen (exakter Abgleich), wirkt nur auf die Erkennung; gefundene Geräte im Reiter Geräte über Gefundenes Gerät hinzufügen übernehmen. -->

Discovery reports everything on the network that answers the search for ESPHome devices. Some routers and other devices answer as well and then appear in the tab "Devices" in the group "Discovered, not added yet". The adapter never connects to discovered devices on its own, they stay there until you add them.
<!-- quelle: code=src/main.ts:316-346@v2.0.0-beta.2; code=src/lib/deviceManagement.ts:338-369@v2.0.0-beta.2; forum=589502; issue=#130; issue=#175 -->

This excludes an address:

1. Open the instance settings, tab "Main settings", section "Discovery".
2. Enter the address in the table "IP addresses to be excluded by device discovery". Name and description are just for you.
3. Save. The instance restarts and skips the address from now on.
<!-- quelle: code=admin/jsonConfig.json5:131-180@v2.0.0-beta.2; code=src/main.ts:291-304@v2.0.0-beta.2 -->

The address must be written exactly as the device reports it. The list only affects discovery, a device you already added keeps connecting. If you do not need discovery at all, switch off "Device discovery enabled" and add devices by hand.
<!-- quelle: code=src/main.ts:318-323@v2.0.0-beta.2; code=src/main.ts:174-187@v2.0.0-beta.2; code=admin/jsonConfig.json5:104-113@v2.0.0-beta.2 -->
