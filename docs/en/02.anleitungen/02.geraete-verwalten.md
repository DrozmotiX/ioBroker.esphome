## How do I delete a device completely and why do the objects come back?

<!-- abschnitt: prio=B; gewicht=6; readme=covered; cluster=connection-setup-16; features=device-delete-sendto,device-credentials-storage,device-startup-reconnect,discovery-new-device-list -->
<!-- muss beantworten: Gerät im Reiter Geräte löschen (mit Rückfrage, endgültig), ESP ausschalten oder Ignorierliste nutzen, sonst taucht es bei der Erkennung wieder auf. -->

Always delete a device in the tab "Devices": choose "Delete device" on the device and confirm. The adapter then disconnects and deletes the device with all its states. This cannot be undone.
<!-- quelle: code=src/lib/deviceManagement.ts:279-286@v2.0.0-beta.2; code=src/lib/deviceManagement.ts:441-456@v2.0.0-beta.2; code=src/main.ts:1684-1702@v2.0.0-beta.2; forum=776659 -->

If you only delete the objects in the tab "Objects" instead, the device stays connected in the running adapter. It creates the objects again on the next connection.
<!-- quelle: code=src/main.ts:493-533@v2.0.0-beta.2; forum=686724; issue=#198 -->

If the device keeps running after deletion, discovery finds it again. It then shows up under "Discovered, not added yet", without states. To keep it from appearing there, switch it off or add its address to the exclusion list of discovery.
<!-- quelle: code=src/main.ts:316-346@v2.0.0-beta.2; issue=#356 -->
