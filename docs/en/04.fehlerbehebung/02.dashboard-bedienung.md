## Why does the OTA update abort or time out?

<!-- abschnitt: prio=B; gewicht=2; readme=missing; cluster=dashboard-22; features=dashboard-enable -->
<!-- muss beantworten: OTA läuft zwischen Dashboard und Gerät; Timeout direkt nach dem Upload ist normal; Netzwerk und Gerät prüfen. -->

The OTA update runs between dashboard and device, the adapter is not involved.
<!-- quelle: code=src/lib/dashboard.ts:613-617@v2.0.0-beta.2; forum=814434 -->

A timeout right after a complete upload is normal. The device restarts with the new firmware and is briefly unreachable, then the adapter connects again.
<!-- quelle: forum=688695; code=src/main.ts:854-918@v2.0.0-beta.2 -->

If the upload stops in the middle, it is almost always the network or the device:

1. Check the Wi-Fi signal of the device. Below about -80 dBm uploads often fail.
2. Check whether a firewall between the ioBroker host and the device blocks the OTA port.
3. If that does not help, flash once by cable.
<!-- quelle: extern=esphome.io/components/ota -->

**See also:** [Flash by cable](../01.erste-schritte/04.dashboard-einschalten.md#flash-device-first-time-if)
